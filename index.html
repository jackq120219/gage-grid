METHOD / DECISION LOGIC

From records to a decision.

A

Normalize

Convert fragmented utility plans, filings, public works records and service notes into comparable infrastructure records.

B

Discount

Usable capacity is discounted by evidence quality, timing, uncertainty and known downstream constraints—not just raw nameplate capacity.

C

Match

Project demand is tested system-by-system. The lowest coverage ratio and highest timing risk determine the site’s practical bottleneck.

D

Direct

The output tells a user what to verify next, which risk matters most, and whether another site deserves capital first.
