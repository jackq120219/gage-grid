METHOD / DECISION LOGIC

From records to a decision.

A

Normalize

Convert fragmented utility plans, filings, public works records and service notes into comparable infrastructure records.

B

Discount

Raw headroom is discounted by evidence quality, confidence, timing and redundancy. Gage Grid shows the uncertainty instead of burying it.

C

Match

Project demand is tested system-by-system, including expected load growth. The weakest dependency and timing risk drive the verdict.

D

Direct

The output quantifies the gap, tells the user what would change the decision and produces an ordered diligence path.
